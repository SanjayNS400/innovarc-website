A little about us

We began by listening to people at home. Now we bring that same care to business.

InnovArc is a design and space-solutions company. Our first five years were shaped by B2C projects and the everyday details that make a place feel right. Today, we’re carrying that people-first perspective into B2B — helping organisations create spaces that support their culture, their teams and their ambitions.
